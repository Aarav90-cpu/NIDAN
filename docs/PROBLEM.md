# The Problem

Classrooms contain students at vastly different levels of understanding. Despite this, they are often taught the exact same material at the exact same pace.

### Key Challenges

1. **Scalability of Assessment:** Teachers cannot manually assess the detailed understanding of every single student daily.
2. **Identification of Gaps:** Identifying exactly what a student understands and pinpointing their specific misconceptions is extremely time-consuming.
3. **Actionable Intervention:** Even when a teacher knows a student is struggling, determining the precise prerequisite concept they need to practice next is difficult to execute at a classroom scale.
4. **Infrastructure Constraints:** Many target classrooms lack reliable, continuous internet access, making cloud-only educational tools unusable.

**The Core Question:** 
How do we efficiently identify what a student understands and automatically determine what they should learn next, without requiring constant internet access?
